REMOTE SERVICES — Zeko Love
===========================

Zeko Love performs no server-side outbound HTTP requests. The only external
service is client-side video embedding for intro videos.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. YouTube / Vimeo intro-video embeds (client-side)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Hosts rendered (browser loads the iframe):
   - https://www.youtube.com/embed/<id>        (from youtube.com/watch?v= or
     youtu.be/ URLs — includes/calls/class-zeko-love-calls.php lines 419-427)
   - https://player.vimeo.com/video/<id>       (from vimeo.com/<id> URLs —
     lines 430-435)
Data sent: when a visitor plays the intro video the provider receives the
           embed request, the visitor's IP address, and interaction data per
           YouTube/Vimeo's own policies. No profile, email, or account data is
           sent by Zeko Love.
When     : only when a listing author saves a YouTube/Vimeo intro URL and a
           visitor opens that listing.

No other outbound requests are made by Zeko Love.
